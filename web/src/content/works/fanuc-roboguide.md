Offline programming of a FANUC 6-axis industrial robot in **ROBOGUIDE**. I taught the arm a sequence of positions over a workpiece and ran the program in simulation. The pink lines are the tool path traced as the robot moves, which shows the reach, approach angles and motion of the program before it ever runs on real hardware.
